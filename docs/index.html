Update 4.5 -

  • Added Credits button
  • Added slider for Misprint Mode to modify amount of randomness
  • Added 1 new gamemode: Natural Disaster Survival
  • Added segments to the player's healthbar to more clearly show how much health the player has
  • Fixed some image scaling issues with devices with higher resolution
  • Balance Changes:
    • Changed weighting of the randomization for Misprint Mode
      • Developer's Note: Originally, the randomness was designed so that the average multiplier that misprint mode would give was 1X. However, this meant that smaller multipliers were much more common than larger ones. The rework makes higher multipliers more likely to appear.
    • Changed default Randomness for Misprint Mode (0.25X-4X -> 0.33X-3X)
      • Developer's Note: You can always change it back to the original value if you want. The old 4x multiplier often led to unwinnable runs where the multiplier made unkillable and supersonic enemies.
    • Decreased enemy spawn rate for the One Shot minigame
    • Time between attacks for enemies with projectiles in the One Shot minigame will increase by 50%
      • Developer's Note: One shot should be extremely difficult, not unfair. Right now, if you get a Collatz Conjecture enemy or an Arena Closer enemy then you almost insta lose. This will (hopefully) still make this minigame really hard but not unfair.
    • Decreased amount of enemies in Boss Rush (Wave 8: 4 -> 3, Wave 10: 5 -> 4, Wave 11: 6 -> 5)
      • Developer's Note: Boss Rush is a little chaotic right now and can lag towards the end of the game. Combined with the fact that boos HP increases in the later waves, having this many bosses is no longer necessary. The distribution between Tier 1 and Tier 2 bosses have also been altered slightly.
    • Buffed "Player takes 1 damage every 4 seconds" punishment in Pick Your Poison
      • Developer's Note: 1 damage every 4 seconds is too light especially considering the player's max health is 2X normal.

    • Arena Closer's bullets will now have a circular hitbox
      • Developer's Note: took long enough for that to happen
    • Increased the amount of time PortalHub's portals last for before disappearing (20s -> 30s)
    • Decreased PortalHub's time between attacks (4s -> 3.5s)
      • Developer's Note: This enemy was a lot less threatening than I expected but I also don't want to keep increasing the health of enemies so these are my balance changes
    • Decreased Calculus enemy's health (35 -> 30)
    • Decreased Calculus enemy's spawn rate
      • Developer's Note: Combined with the two enemies that it spawns on death, Calculus had a lot of health and was very hard to deal with without high dps.
    • Decreased spawn rate for all enemies for waves 8, 9, 10, and 11
      • Developer's Note: As mentioned in previous updates, I want waves to have less enemy spam and more individually difficult enemies. We now have more difficult enemies so we can reduce the amount of them.

Update 2.0 - July 6, 2026

  • Added new feature: Unlockable characters! These are chosen before the start of the game and can be unlocked though progression. Currently, there are three new characters: Tank, Healer, and Mage. Each character has their own unique strengths and weaknesses.
  • Added 1 new tier 3 enemy
  • Balance Changes:
    • Increased time between attacks of arena closer (0.33s -> 0.5s)
      • Developer's note: Arena closer was too good at its job.
    • Increased health of slither.io boss (240 -> 300)
      • Developer's note: slither.io could be burst down too easily, especially for a tier 2 boss.
    • Increased starting health hultiplier for easy and medium difficulties (1.5x -> 1.8x for easy, 1.2x -> 1.4x for medium)
    • Decreased enemy spawn rate for easy and medium difficulties.
      • Developer's note: Since there are now multiple characters with different amounts of starting health, it has become a multiplier instead of a flat increase. Easy and medium mode were still too difficult so they get nerfed yet again.
    • Made every boss enemy deal knockback to the player instead of instantly killing them on contact
      • Developer's note: Instakilling the player on contact caused a lot of runs to suddenly die out, and it's always really frustrating when that happens. Now every boss deals knockback and a little bit of damage instead. All the bosses currently in the game either already deal knockback or rely on their projectiles for damage, so this shouldn't be too big of a nerf.
    • Made health potions survive longer before disappearing (10s -> 16.67s)

Update 1.5 - July 3, 2026

  • Added 1 new tier 3 enemy
  • Added 1 new upgrade
  • Made intro screen look slightly better
  • Added background color
  • Balance Changes:
    • Increased health of furnace zombie (15 -> 20)
    • Reworked speed scaling for furnace zombie (Before: 6-health/3. After: 6.5-health/4)
      • Developer's note: Furnace zombie felt way too underpowered for a tier 3 enemy.
    • Increased the rate at which health potions spawn (16.67s on average -> 10s)
    • Increased the range of healing amounts that health potions can give (1-4 -> 2-5)
      • Developer's note: Health potions felt too rare and not useful enough, leading to players being forced to pick healing-related upgrades in order to stay alive.
    • Decreased amount of times the 2x Exp Gain upgrade could be obtained (2 -> 1)
    • Nerfed wave 9 (2 tier 3 enemies -> 1 tier 3 enemy)
    • Nerfed wave 10 (3 tier 3 enemies -> 2 tier 3 enemies)
      • Developer's note: Waves 9 and 10 were still too difficult even on easy difficulty.
